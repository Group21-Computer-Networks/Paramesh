### 2026-10-18 — session 1

- Started the task. Read the card, the milestone's Streams and Gate text, and AGENTS.md.
- `src/tools/top.cpp` (new): `pm top [--http-port N]` switches to the terminal's own screen
  and hides the cursor, then once a second clears and draws: the node and the time, and for
  each job on the node its role, launcher, slots, faults per second, MB per second in and
  out, and fault latency (p50, p99), with a line on page ownership (segments homed here,
  pages held S and M), from GET /v1/jobs and /v1/jobs/{id}/pages. Ctrl+C or SIGTERM ends it:
  the cursor and the normal screen come back. A pmd that does not answer is said on the
  screen, and it keeps trying.
- `src/tools/pm.{h,cpp}`: `top` beside `status` and `leave`.
- Test (`tests/unit/pmd_test.cpp`) "pm top redraws once a second during a run and gives the
  terminal back on Ctrl+C": during a run of the test program, `pm top` for 3.5 s, then SIGINT:
  it starts with the screen and cursor codes, draws 3 to 5 frames, shows the job and its pages
  line, ends with the codes that restore them, and exits 0.
- Commands: the test, three runs: passed. clang-tidy: three findings (a global flag, the clock,
  a `const_cast` in the test), fixed, exit 0. clang-format: clean.
