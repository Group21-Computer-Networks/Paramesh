### 2026-10-14 — session 1

- Started the task. Read the card, the milestone's Streams and Gate text, and AGENTS.md.
- Read what exists: the transport already sends `HEARTBEAT` every 1 s, reports 3 s of
  silence, a reset, and a request unanswered after one retry (M1-5, `net_test`); the job
  process already turns each into `JOB_END` from the launcher with the status and the node,
  and every process prints `job N aborted: ...` and exits (M1-9, `lib_test`).
- What had no test: the whole path under `pmrun` and three daemons. Wrote two cases; both
  passed with no change to `src/lib/` or `src/pmd/`.
- So this task adds tests only. The home store and the spill file are inside the job
  process (the file is unlinked when made, M4-3), so they are freed when the process ends
  and the daemon reaps it.
- The silence case's lower bound was first written as 2.0 s and changed to 1.9 s before it
  was ever committed or had failed: the 3 s run from the node's last frame, which can be a
  heartbeat up to 1 s old, so 2.0 s is the least possible and no margin.
- State: Done-when met.

Done-when, local, three simulated nodes (`tests/unit/pmd_test.cpp`;
`unit_pmd_test -tc="killing a node*,a node that goes silent*"`). A job of three nodes started
by `pmrun`, its chunks running:
- "killing a node mid-run ends the job everywhere within 3 s, naming the node": one worker
  gets SIGKILL. `pmrun` ended with status 1 after 0.20 s in each of five runs (`build/dev`)
  and 0.30 s under AddressSanitizer (`build/m46-clang-address`); stderr had
  `job 65537 aborted: node 2 lost`; no process of the job was left; the program wrote no
  result.
- "a node that goes silent ends the job after 3 s without a heartbeat": one worker gets
  SIGSTOP. `pmrun` ended with status 1 after 2.60 to 2.70 s in five runs and 2.80 s under
  AddressSanitizer, with the same message; the other worker and the launcher were gone. The
  stopped process had to be killed by the test (see Deferred).
- The second 2 s reply timeout: `net_test`, "a timed request is sent again with RETRY after
  2 s and reported after 4 s", unchanged and passing.

Quality gate, local:
- Unit tests, GCC and Clang, each without a sanitizer and with address, undefined and thread
  (`build/m46-<cc>-<san>`): 100% passed in all eight, 16 of 16 (15 of 15 under TSan). No
  sanitizer report and no assertion message.
- `cmake --build --preset tidy`: exit 0. clang-format check as CI runs it: clean.
