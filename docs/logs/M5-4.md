### 2026-10-16 — session 1

- Started the task. Read the card, the milestone's Streams and Gate text, and AGENTS.md.
- Asked the person: `LEDGER_SYNC`'s codec belongs in `src/wire/`, outside the card. Approved.
- `src/wire/`: `LEDGER_SYNC` (one 40-byte entry per job), with a wire test. `HELLO`'s totals
  codec already existed.
- `src/pmd/ledger_status.{h,cpp}` (new): this node's raw totals with a peer, and per job
  (chunks, and segment-seconds rounded down); the alarm rule (two counts differ by more than
  10% of the larger, and by more than 1, which is rounding); and the JSON of GET /v1/ledger:
  per peer this node's totals, balance, weight and pending indexes, what the peer announced
  and whether it disagrees, and the same per job with the credits. What peers announce is
  kept apart from the ledger and goes into nothing else.
- `pmd`: `HELLO` carries this node's totals with up to 7 peers; a `HELLO`'s entry about this
  node is kept. When a job's process here ends, `LEDGER_SYNC` with this node's counts for the
  job goes to the other side: from a worker's node to the launcher's `pmd` (on the
  `SPAWN_REQ`'s connection), from the launcher's to each member's (on the connection its
  `SPAWN_REQ` went on). Once a second the alarm is worked out (logged `ledger_disagreement`
  once for each peer, or peer and job, that starts to differ) and the JSON remade under a
  mutex, which the status API's thread serves.
- `pm status` adds a ledger table from /v1/ledger: per peer, chunks and segment-seconds each
  way, balance, weight, and whether the announcement agrees.
- A problem found by the cross-daemon test under load: a `LEDGER_SYNC` from the launcher's
  node can arrive before the worker's last `L_CHUNK`s on the local socket have been read, so
  comparing at once could raise a false alarm. Announcements are now only stored when they
  arrive and compared at the once-a-second refresh.
- Tests: `wire_test` `LEDGER_SYNC`; `pmd_test` "raw counts disagree past 10% (and past
  rounding), and totals are this node's own"; "forged totals on HELLO are shown and flagged,
  and move no weight" (node 8 claims a billion chunks for node 5: /v1/ledger shows it,
  `disagree` true, balance 0, weight 1, and `pm status` prints "DISAGREES by more than 10%");
  "after a job both daemons' /v1/ledger agree, and LEDGER_SYNC says so" (node 1's count of
  node 2's chunks equals node 2's own, both announced the same, no disagreement).
- Commands: those cases, three runs: passed. `ctest --test-dir build/dev -j4`: 16 of 16
  twice after the fix (once before it, the cross-daemon case failed under load: the cause
  above). clang-tidy: a static's name, and `hear()` too complex; fixed, exit 0.

## Decisions

<!--
Choices you made inside the card's scope, each with the reason. Not for points listed under
"Still open" in the plan: those are asked, never decided here. Write "None" if there were none.
-->
- **"Matches the task log"** is read as: what the launcher's daemon counted of a peer's
  chunks (from the job's `L_CHUNK` reports) equals what the peer's daemon counted of its own,
  and each side announced the same; the cross-daemon test checks that.
- **A gap of 1 is never an alarm,** besides the card's 10%: segment-seconds are whole seconds
  and each side stops counting when its own process ends.
- **`HELLO` carries the first 7 peers of the ledger,** the most the payload holds; which 7 is
  not chosen further.
- **Announcements are compared once a second, not on arrival** (the ordering above).
