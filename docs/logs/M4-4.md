### 2026-10-14 — session 2: admission

- `src/wire/`: `LAdmitRefusedPayload` and its codec (the opcode existed, the payload did not).
- `src/pmd/pmd.cpp`:
  - on `L_ADMIT_REQ` the peers to ask are those heard through discovery and not leaving,
    most free RAM and spill first, then the seeds of `--peers` not heard yet;
  - if the peers that would be chosen have all been heard and the region is more than 90% of
    their announced RAM and spill plus this node's, `L_ADMIT_REFUSED` goes out before any
    `SPAWN_REQ`, with the needed and available bytes and a sentence to print;
  - when the answers are in, the same check is made on what the members committed in
    `SPAWN_OK` (fewer may have accepted, or a seed was not known before);
  - `SPAWN_OK` now carries this node's RAM cap and spill budget, and `HELLO` its spill budget;
  - `spill.budget` for the daemon: `PARAMESH_CFG_SPILL_BUDGET`, else the RAM cap, read in
    `open()`;
  - a node with no RAM cap (`cap_ram` 0, which only a test's configuration can give) takes
    any region.
- `src/lib/job.cpp`: `pm_init()` prints `job N refused: <the sentence>` before returning
  `PM_ERR_REFUSED`.
- Tests: three cases in `pmd_test`, the payload in `wire_test`. Two mistakes of mine on the
  way, both in new test code: a hex string with double spaces, and a first wording of the
  message that counted the launcher alone; the message now names the nodes chosen.
- State: Done-when met.
