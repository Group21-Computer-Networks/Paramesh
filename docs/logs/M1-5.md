### 2026-10-07 — session 1

- Started after PR #12 (M1-4) merged; `main` at `8d81fe9`. Finishes stream B.
- Plan: one program, `tests/sim/fuzz.cpp`. It runs the real `coh_step` and `home_step` on three
  simulated nodes joined by a fake bus, picks every next move from a seeded generator, and
  checks the invariants of `docs/STATE_MACHINES.md` section 4 after every step.
- The M1 machines refuse writes to a page others hold, so the workload is the M1 story: a
  page's writer writes first, readers come after.
- Next: the program, its two ctest entries, then 2,000 schedules.

### 2026-10-07 — session 2: built and run locally

- `tests/sim/fuzz.cpp` (about 370 lines) and `tests/sim/CMakeLists.txt`: the `sim_fuzz` program
  and two ctest entries, `sim.fuzz` (`--schedules 2000`) and `sim.fuzz_self_test`.
- A schedule: 3 nodes, 3 pages (one written by node 2, one by the home's own node, one never
  written), 300 random moves (read fault, write fault, write, or deliver the head of a randomly
  chosen queue), then everything in transit is delivered and no node may still be waiting.
- The bus keeps one queue per ordered pair of nodes and never reorders within a queue, as TCP
  does not; delay and reordering are between queues. The machines rely on that order.
- Invariants checked after every step: one writer or many readers; no stale read; the home
  knows every holder; no lost update; a node's state matches its mapping. Any abort action
  from either machine also fails the schedule.
- Random numbers are `std::mt19937_64` reduced with `%`, not a standard distribution, so a
  seed gives the same schedule under every compiler and library.
- Local result: 2,000 schedules pass in 0.5 s. Self-test: with the planted bug (the home does
  not keep what it fetched) seed 1 fails at step 105, "the home was to send a copy it does not
  have"; the same seed fails identically again and passes without the bug.
- Next: push; the Done-when names Actions, so the task is marked done only after the run.

## Decisions

<!--
Choices you made inside the card's scope, each with the reason. Not for points listed under
"Still open" in the plan: those are asked, never decided here. Write "None" if there were none.
-->
- **One program with a plain `main`, no test framework:** it has to take a seed on the command
  line to reproduce a failure.
- **Order is kept within a connection.** The card says "reorders"; the harness reorders between
  connections only, because the protocol is specified over TCP and its "impossible" pairs rest
  on per-connection order.
- **The planted bug lives in the harness,** behind `--self-test`: it shows the checker can fail
  and that a seed reproduces, without touching `src/coh/`.
- **The workload stays inside what M1 supports** (writer first, then readers). M2-6 widens it.
