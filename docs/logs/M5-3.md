### 2026-10-16 — session 1

- Started the task. Read the card, the milestone's Streams and Gate text, and AGENTS.md.
- Asked the person: a job ignored an `L_QUOTA` after start and the task queue could not
  change its thread count, so obeying a pushed quota needs `src/lib/job.cpp` and
  `src/rt/tasks.{h,cpp}`, outside the card. Approved (below).
- `src/rt/tasks.{h,cpp}`: `set_quota(n)`: only threads 0 to n - 1 ask for chunks; a thread
  above it runs what it already holds, then waits for the quota to rise: the change takes
  effect at a task boundary.
- `src/pmd/scheduler.{h,cpp}` (new): `slot_weight()` = max(0.25, 1 + balance / scale) from a
  `LedgerView`, and `allocate_slots()`: a job that went without a slot at the last share-outs
  gets one first, the longest waiting first; then this node's own jobs take what they want;
  the rest go one at a time to the guest with the lowest dominant share / weight (the larger
  of its part of the slots and of the home-store RAM its segments here take).
- `pmd`: each worker and launcher here is a job with its bound (`threads_per_node`), its
  share and its wait; the slots are shared out again at every `L_CHUNK` (a task boundary),
  `L_REGISTER`, `L_ADMIT_REQ`, `L_SEGMENTS` and closed connection, and every process whose
  share changed is sent `L_QUOTA`; a launcher not before its `L_ADMIT_OK`, which it reads as
  its next frame. `ledger.scale` and `mem.cache_share` come from `PARAMESH_CFG_*`.
- Job process: starts as many threads as it may ever use (its bound, or one a processor) and
  sets the quota before they start; a later `L_QUOTA` changes it.
- Bugs found on the way, both in my new code and caught by tests: the allocator served jobs
  left out in list order, so with one slot and three jobs the third never had a turn (now the
  longest waiting first); and the quota was set just after the threads started, so they had
  already asked for chunks above it (`over_quota=12` in M3-7's test; now set before).
- A test expectation changed, as the person decided (below): M3-7's "pmrun starts a job on

## Decisions

<!--
Choices you made inside the card's scope, each with the reason. Not for points listed under
"Still open" in the plan: those are asked, never decided here. Write "None" if there were none.
-->
- **The slots are shared out again at every task boundary,** taken as every `L_CHUNK` from
  a process here, not when a particular slot frees: the result is the same allocation, and
  it needs no record of which thread freed.
- **"Has work" is "has registered and not closed":** `pmd` does not see a job's queue.
- **A job wants no more than its bound** (`threads_per_node`, or `--cap-cores` without one);
  what it cannot use goes to the others.
- **The one-boundary rule:** with fewer slots than jobs a job can wait as many share-outs as
  there are jobs ahead of it; the test checks that three jobs on one slot never wait more than
  two in a row.
- **The home-store RAM of a job** is its segments homed here x 2 MB over `--cap-ram` less the
  cache's share; 0 without a cap.
