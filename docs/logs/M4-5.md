## Card

<!-- Copied from docs/PLAN.md when this log was created. Do not edit; the plan is the source. -->
- Goal: Graceful leave of a worker, by `pm leave` or Ctrl+C on its `pmd`: `LEAVE_INTENT`; no new tasks; running tasks get 10 s, then return to the queue; modified pages written back; shared pages dropped; each home segment frozen (`BUSY_RETRY`), drained and sent with `SEG_MIGRATE`; new `SEG_MAP` at a higher epoch; old-epoch requests get `REDIRECT`; `LEAVE_DONE`, then `BYE`. On a launcher, `pm leave` is refused while its job runs and `pm leave --force` aborts the job.
- Files: `src/coh/`, `src/rt/`, `src/pmd/`, `src/store/`, `src/tools/`
- Needs: M4-1, M4-2, M4-3
- Stream: seq
- Done when: A worker leaves during matrix multiply; the job finishes and the result equals the single-machine result; no task is lost.

### 2026-10-14 — session 4: part 3, pmd, pm leave and Ctrl+C

- `pmd` (`src/pmd/pmd.{h,cpp}`, `main.cpp`): `L_LEAVE_REQ` from `pm leave`; the node tells
  the launcher's daemon of every job it serves (`LEAVE_INTENT`, passed on to the launcher
  as `L_LEAVE_INTENT`) and its own workers (`L_LEAVE`); from then on its beacons carry the
  leaving flag, and it declines new runs and spawns. It says `BYE` and exits once its
  workers have gone. On a node that launched a running job `pm leave` is refused, naming
  the job; `pm leave --force` ends that job (`L_ABORT`, "node N is leaving") and then
  leaves. The first Ctrl+C on `pmd` is the same leave with reason 2; a second, or SIGTERM,
  stops at once.

### 2026-10-16 — session 6 (fix): Ctrl+C on pmd could be lost or hit the wrong thread

- Found under ThreadSanitizer while checking M5-2: "Ctrl+C on pmd is a graceful leave"
  failed once (pmd exited 66, TSan's status for a report), and by hand a pmd given two quick
  SIGINTs right after its socket appeared sometimes never left. Cause: `main.cpp` installed
  the handler only after `open()`, which creates the socket and starts the daemon's other
  threads. A signal in between was taken by the default action or dropped by the handler
  (`running()` not set yet), and one delivered to another thread waited there; the handler's
  `write()` could also change `errno` under the code it interrupted.
- Fix (`src/pmd/main.cpp`): SIGINT and SIGTERM are blocked before any thread starts and
  unblocked in the main thread once the handler is installed, so one that comes early waits
  and is then delivered; they are blocked again before `running()` is cleared. The handler
  saves and restores `errno`.
- Commands: a ThreadSanitizer pmd, started, sent two SIGINTs as soon as its socket appeared,
  40 times: before the fix 3 of 10 went wrong (exit 130 twice, killed by the signal; 66
  once); after it 40 of 40 exited 0 with no report. `unit_pmd_test -tc="Ctrl+C on pmd*,pm
  leave on a node*"`: passed. clang-tidy: exit 0. clang-format: clean.

## Decisions

<!--
Choices you made inside the card's scope, each with the reason. Not for points listed under
"Still open" in the plan: those are asked, never decided here. Write "None" if there were none.
-->
- **Which state a segment is in (`OTHER`, `SERVING`, `FROZEN`, `MOVED`, `ARRIVING`) is kept
  by the caller,** in `src/lib/`, as the frozen interface has it: `src/coh/` offers the
  steps (freeze, busy, import, remove).
- **`SEG_MIGRATE`'s decoded entries point into the frame;** nothing is copied until the
  store takes the page.
- **Two maps for one leave,** as the documents read together: `SEG_MAP`'s `LEAVING` flag is
  "named for the last time; it hosts no segment in this map", and `LEAVE_DONE` is answered
  "once every remaining process has acknowledged the map without the leaver". So epoch N+1
  names the leaver with no segment, and after `LEAVE_DONE` epoch N+2 does not name it; a
  process drops its copyset bit then, as `SEG_MAP` says. Section 8.2, step 7, says only
  "the new epoch"; this reading was not put to the person and is named here for review.
- **The leaver and the launcher start independently,** as with two daemons: neither waits
  for the other. The leaver may write pages back before or after its segments freeze; what
  meets a frozen or arriving segment is sent again.
- **A segment is sent 32 pages to a frame,** not the 254 a frame may carry, so that at most
  128 KB of page data is outside the store's cap at a time (marked `ponytail:`).
- **After `REDIRECT` the request is sent again after the back-off,** not exactly when the
  map of the epoch it names is applied; if the old map is still the newest here it is
  redirected once more (marked `ponytail:`).
- **`PARAMESH_TEST_MOVE_DELAY_MS`** is read by the library and is for tests only.
