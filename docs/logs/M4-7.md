### 2026-10-15 — session 1

- Started the task. Read the card, the milestone's Streams and Gate text, and AGENTS.md.
  The sequence is docs/PROTOCOL.md 8.3: the launcher's `pmd` sends `SPAWN_REQ` with
  `MID_RUN` to a newly seen peer and passes the new member to the launcher with `L_MEMBER`;
  the launcher sends `SEG_MAP` at the next epoch with it flagged `NO_HOMES`; no segment
  moves; after its `SEG_MAP_ACK` the new node sends `TASK_REQ`.
- `src/wire/`: `L_MEMBER` (one member entry) and the `MID_RUN` flag, under the M4 payload
  rule; a wire test.
- `pmd`: a job is short when it has fewer members than `pmrun -n` asked for, which happens
  when a member's `LEAVE_INTENT` passes through (it is taken out of the job's members). A
  peer heard for the first time, and not leaving, is offered every short job
  (`SPAWN_REQ` with `MID_RUN`); its `SPAWN_OK` becomes `L_MEMBER` to the launcher. Logged as
  `refill`.
- Job process (launcher): `L_MEMBER` queues the node; when the job is up and no leave is
  under way it becomes a member with a new slot, one above the highest used (a slot is never
  reused), and the map naming it, `NO_HOMES` and weight 0, goes out once it has connected.
  When every member has acknowledged that map it logs `member_joined`. The new worker needs
  nothing new: its first map comes at a later epoch, it connects to every member it names,
  acknowledges, and pulls tasks; the queue serves any node that asks.
- Test (`tests/unit/pmd_test.cpp`), the Done-when. First written with matrix multiply:
  three daemons run `matmul 2048 1`, node 3 leaves, a fourth daemon starts with node 1 as
  its seed; the result must equal `matmul --plain 2048` and node 4 must have run chunks. It
  passed five of five in the dev build ("chunks by node: 1:7 2:4 4:2", 7.8 to 8.2 s), but
  failed under AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer: the job ended (11 s) before
  node 4 had joined. Timed with real daemons and logs: the join took 4.5 s in the dev build,
  most of it hashing the 21 MB debug binary twice (the new node's daemon before it spawns,
  then the worker itself), about 2.3 s each; in docs/TODO.md. A fixed-size job cannot wait
  for that, so the test now runs the test program of `pmd_test` in a new `join` mode: when
  node 3 has begun a chunk it says so, the test makes node 3 leave and starts node 4, and
  rounds of 64 chunks of 2 ms go on until node 4 has run some (at most 120 s). The last
  round's squares are checked, the launcher must log `member_left` and `member_joined`, and
  node 4's count of indexes must be above 0. The check is the same; only the job's length
  no longer depends on how fast a node joins.
- Commands: `build/dev/tests/unit/unit_pmd_test -tc="after a worker leaves*"` three times:
  passed, node 4 ran 6,144 to 9,216 indexes (12 to 18 chunks). The same in
  `build/m47-gcc-address` twice: passed, 7,680 and 9,216. clang-format: clean.
- Also here, as the person chose on 2026-10-15: the AT-1 test that depends on timing is
  recorded in docs/TODO.md (no change to it).

## Decisions

<!--
Choices you made inside the card's scope, each with the reason. Not for points listed under
"Still open" in the plan: those are asked, never decided here. Write "None" if there were none.
-->
- **Short means fewer members than `pmrun -n` asked for,** counted by the launcher's `pmd`,
  which learns of a leave from the `LEAVE_INTENT` it relays. A job that started short
  because peers declined ([GATE P8]) is refilled the same way.
- **"Newly seen" is a peer's first `HELLO` (`peer_new`),** as the card says; a peer known and
  idle when the job became short is not offered it (in docs/TODO.md).
- **A joiner's slot is one above the highest slot used,** so a leaver's slot is never given
  to another ([GATE P5]).
- **One join at a time, and none while a leave is under way:** the leave's two maps and the
  join's map are not interleaved. A leave that starts while a join map is out is fine: its
  map names the new member too.
