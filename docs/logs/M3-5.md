- `src/pmd/pmd.cpp`, for a job launched on this node:
  - `L_RUN_REQ` from `pmrun`: a job ID, this node's ID in the high half and a counter kept in
    the state directory in the low half.
  - `L_REGISTER` from the launcher `pmrun` starts: it becomes the job's first member.
  - `L_ADMIT_REQ`: `SPAWN_REQ` to the peers in the order of `--peers`, for N-1 workers. A peer
    that declines, cannot be reached or hangs up is replaced by the next. When no request is
    outstanding the launcher gets `L_ADMIT_OK` with its quota and the members.
  - `pmrun`'s connection closing while the job runs: `L_ABORT`, status `USER_ABORT`, to the
    launcher.
- `src/tools/pmrun.cpp` and the `pmrun` executable: `pmrun [-n nodes] [--state-dir DIR]
  program [arguments...]`. It hashes the program, asks the daemon for a job, starts the
  program as the launcher with the environment of section 10, and exits with its status. On
  SIGINT or SIGTERM it lets go of the daemon, waits up to 5 s for the launcher, and exits 130.
- Tests: three daemons in one test process, the real `pmrun` executable, and this test
  executable as a stand-in for every job process.
- A wrong expectation of mine in the first test: the members after the launcher come in the

## Decisions

<!--
Choices you made inside the card's scope, each with the reason. Not for points listed under
"Still open" in the plan: those are asked, never decided here. Write "None" if there were none.
-->
- **Peers are asked in the order of `--peers`,** not by "most free capacity": a daemon learns
  a peer's capacity from `HELLO`, which is M4-1.
- **Fewer than N nodes:** when the peers run out the job is admitted with those that
  accepted, as the M0 gate settled ([GATE P8]). `pmrun` does not print how many it got: no
  message tells it. In `docs/TODO.md`.
- **No capacity check and no `L_ADMIT_REFUSED`:** admission by capacity is M4-4.
- **Ctrl+C is carried by the connection, not by a new message:** `pmrun` keeps its connection
  to the daemon for the life of the job, and closing it is the signal. The protocol has
  `L_ABORT` from the daemon to the launcher and nothing from `pmrun` to the daemon.
- **Workers are not killed by their daemons.** As section 8.4 has it, the launcher tells every
  process with `JOB_END`, and a worker that loses the launcher ends by itself. The stand-in
  workers do the second.
- **A job ends, for the daemon, when its launcher's connection closes.** `L_JOB_END` is not
  read yet.
- **One connection for each `SPAWN_REQ`, and a blocking connect.** Marked `ponytail:`.
- **The launcher's own member entry has address 0** (it knows itself), and every `ram_weight`
  is 0 until daemons commit memory (M4-2). M3-7 has to take both into account.
- **`pmrun` links the daemon's library** for the two frame functions (`src/pmd/frame_io.h`).
